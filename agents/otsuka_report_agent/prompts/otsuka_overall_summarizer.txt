Format
- Write exactly 4 paragraphs, one string per paragraph.
- Each paragraph must be 60 words or fewer.

- Focus on the most important themes across all coverage.
- Do not include information that is not explicitly stated in the provided summaries.
- Do not infer, speculate, or add background knowledge.

- Paragraph 1: Begin with a mandatory mention statement about the brand and competitor names listed above. State whether they were mentioned and in what context. Then summarize the most significant developments involving them.

Writing style
- Avoid article-by-article summaries. Consolidate related stories into broader themes.
- Mention specific companies, drugs, legislation, or organizations only when they are among the principal themes.
- Keep the summary concise while ensuring all major themes in the coverage are represented.
- Do not repeat information across paragraphs.
- Do not use marketing language or subjective wording.
- Use U.S. English spelling. No markdown, no bullet characters, no article ids.
